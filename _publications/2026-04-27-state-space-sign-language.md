Introduced PHONSSM, a sign language recognition model enforcing phonological decomposition through anatomically-grounded graph attention and orthogonal subspace factorization. Achieved 72.1% on WLASL2000 using skeleton data alone (+18.4pp over prior state-of-the-art), with a +225% relative gain in the few-shot regime and zero-shot transfer to ASL Citizen.
