Introduced PhonSSM, a sign language recognition model enforcing phonological decomposition through anatomically-grounded graph attention and orthogonal subspace factorization. Achieved state-of-the-art results on various datasets using skeleton data alone, with a +225% relative gain in the few-shot regime and zero-shot transfer to ASL Citizen.
