Conducted the first systematic study of target-conditional molecular property prediction across 10 protein families and 4 fusion architectures using NestDrug, a FiLM-based conditioning model. Showed that FiLM outperforms concatenation by 24.2pp and that context enables otherwise-impossible predictions in data-scarce regimes (0.686 AUC vs. 0.238 for per-target Random Forest on CYP3A4).
